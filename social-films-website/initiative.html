The model

How we fund grassroots storytelling

Frontline community organizations often do the most critical work with the fewest communications resources. Without professional visual storytelling, they struggle to compete for grants or corporate partnerships. The Social Films Initiative removes that barrier. It runs separately from our studio work, and is funded three ways.

  1. 1
    10% of Social Films’ profits

    10% of the profits from our commissioned studio work goes directly to the Initiative.

  2. 2
    Brand & foundation co-sponsorships

    Purpose-driven brands and foundations sponsor specific community stories or regional cohorts, gaining transparent impact reporting while funding local leaders.

  3. 3
    Grants

    Grant support allows us to scale our qualitative archiving and expand pro-bono capacity to underserved regions.

Together, they fund
Community-level documentaries, fully sponsored for the organizations we film

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Agile, cinema-grade execution. Zero-friction crews.

Traditional film crews can overwhelm vulnerable communities and inflate budgets. We operate with a nimble, cinema-grade documentary footprint, combining broadcast-quality visuals, aerial cinematography and intimate interview techniques that put real people at ease while keeping resources focused on impact.

Ethical storytelling charter

Exchange over extraction.

Full asset ownership

Grassroots partners receive full licensing and unrestricted use of their finished films for fundraising, grants and outreach.

Dignity & agency

Communities co-review narratives to ensure accuracy, safety and authentic representation, never poverty tropes.

Long-term capacity

We deliver modular video cuts and transcripts built to serve organizations long after wrap day.
