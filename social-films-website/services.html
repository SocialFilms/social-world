Social Films

Documentaries & docu-series

Every film carries the prestige of a national broadcast. We provide end-to-end support, from strategic content planning and interviewing diverse voices to collaborative editing. Whether it's a multi-part series or a singular brand story, we transform complex visions into human-centric narratives.

02
The mission film

A dedicated film your organization keeps, built to grow awareness and power your fundraising: for donors, grant applications, events and your website.

03
Voices of your community

The people you serve and the people doing the work, on camera, in their own words. Authentic interviews that help your organization own its narrative and build trust.

04
Awareness content

Vertical cuts, reels and shorts that carry your mission into social feeds, events and campaigns.

05
Post-production support

Already have footage from the field? We find the story and finish it, from editing to color, sound and platform-ready delivery.

06
Training for your team

Training and consultation on video storytelling, so your in-house team can keep telling your story between shoots.

OUR PROMISE

10% of our profits go back to the communities we film

Every project, every client

From every project, 10% of our profits fund the Social Films Initiative, our program for community-led documentaries. Your budget helps put the people driving change on screen.
